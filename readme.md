# My Digital Space - Enes ÇAKIR Portfolio

Welcome! This is the repository for my personal portfolio website. It's more than just a list of my experiences; it's a piece of my digital identity where I showcase my journey as a developer, the tools I love working with, and the projects I'm passionate about.

## Why This Site?

I built this space to bridge the gap between a traditional resume and my actual work. It’s designed to give you a quick look at:
- **My Journey:** From my high school roots in software development to my current studies and professional steps.
- **My Toolbox:** The tech stack I use to bring ideas to life.
- **What I'm Building:** A spotlight on my latest ventures, including projects like YetLove.
- **Connection:** Making it easy for like-minded developers or potential collaborators to reach out.

## The Tech Behind the Scenes

I wanted the site to be fast, responsive, and visually clean. Here’s what I used to build it:

- **Bootstrap 5:** For a solid, mobile-first responsive foundation.
- **Modern CSS:** I used CSS variables, custom animations, and subtle glow effects to give it a modern, dark-themed aesthetic.
- **Vanilla JavaScript:** Keeping things lightweight for smooth scrolling and interactive elements.
- **Google Fonts & Font Awesome:** Using the 'Inter' typeface and vector icons for a sharp, professional look.

## Project Structure

## How to View It

Since this is a static site, you don't need to install anything. Just download the files and open `index.html` in your favorite browser.
